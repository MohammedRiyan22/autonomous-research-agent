# Example report 3: Unanswered made-up product

**Question:** What are the published clinical-trial results for the Zorblax home fusion battery?

**Research plan:** Search the exact product name and variants; look for an identifiable manufacturer, clinical-trial registry, regulator, or peer-reviewed paper; report only traceable evidence.

**Example result when no reliable sources are found:** “I could not find reliable published clinical-trial results for a product by this name in the sources located. This does not prove that no such product or study exists. The search may miss private, newly published, or differently named material.”

**Uncertainty:** The made-up name is intentionally fictional. If the live search returns unrelated matches, the app should list them as irrelevant/weak evidence rather than treating their presence as proof. Search-service coverage is incomplete.

**Purpose:** Demonstrates an honest non-answer and the difference between “not found” and “does not exist.”
