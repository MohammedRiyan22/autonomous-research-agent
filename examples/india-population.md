# Example report 2: Population estimate disagreement

**Question:** How many people live in India? Show the dates if sources give different numbers.

**Research plan:** Compare official estimates and record each estimate's reference year and publication basis.

**Short answer:** The two cited UN products give different estimates for different reference years. Both should be reported with their year and source.

**Important claims:**

- The UN World Statistics Pocketbook 2025 lists **1,463,866 thousand (about 1.464 billion) for 2025**. [UN World Statistics Pocketbook 2025](https://unstats.un.org/UNSDWebsite/publications/pocketbook/world-stats-pocketbook-2025.pdf)
- The UN World Population Prospects 2024 summary lists **1,444,436 thousand (about 1.444 billion) for 2024**. [UN World Population Prospects 2024: Summary of Results](https://population.un.org/wpp/assets/Files/WPP2024_Summary-of-Results.pdf)

**Disagreement / uncertainty:** These are differing estimates, but not a like-for-like contradiction: the reference years and statistical products differ. The sample does not establish which figure is best for every use. Both are estimates/projections rather than current census counts.

**Purpose:** Demonstrates how the app should preserve differing numbers, dates, and methods instead of collapsing them into one unsupported value. Re-check the sources before presenting because estimates may be revised.
